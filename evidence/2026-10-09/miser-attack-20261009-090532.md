## EXPLOIT — broke + fixed (1, both paths)

**E1. Cache burial laundered diseaseRisk + poisonRisk (REAL, measured 0.35
sick chance -> 0).** `buryCache`'s food branch pushed a subset shape —
`{name, kcalEach, units, spoilDay, safe, kg, unit}` — silently dropping
`diseaseRisk`, `poisonRisk`, `needsCooking`, `foodState`, `foodKind`,
`hiddenKcal`, `rawKcal`, `prep`, `wellMade`, `burnt`, `edible`, `plantId`.
Bury raw risky meat, dig it up, eat it clean: a free disease bypass for the
price of 48 ticks instead of finding fire. Worse: poisoned meat came back
unpoisoned — toxin laundering straight into the village's mouths. Both the
full dig-up AND the ration-drawer portion path were affected (same push).
Fix (design call, "figure it out yourself" authority): the burial push now
carries the full pantryAdd/takenStack field contract — burial is a
round-trip, what goes into the earth comes back unchanged. Risk objects are
shallow-copied so a partial bury doesn't alias the pack stack left behind.
Also carried `bonded`/`keepsake`/`stolen` so burying a sentimental item
doesn't un-bond it on the way back up. Post-fix: buried meat keeps
diseaseRisk 0.35, poisonRisk, needsCooking, foodState 'cleaned' through
bury -> dig and bury -> drawer alike.

## HELD (attacked, resisted)

- **Sign exploits**: donateMaterial/takeMaterial/buryCache/takeFromCache
  with negative qty, zero qty, and spendMaterial(-n) all refuse or no-op
  with zero state change. Float qty floors honestly (3.7 -> 3).
- **Overweight dig softlock**: digUpCache refuses with "Too heavy" and the
  cache SURVIVES (nothing destroyed); the ration drawer is the working
  escape hatch — one log drawable when the whole stockpile isn't. The
  miser promise (winter stockpile you draw from) holds under a full pack.
- **Location gate**: dig/take from the wrong node refuse, cite the journal,
  cache untouched.
- **Spoilage underground**: all-spoiled cache resolves honestly (worms get
  it, cache removed, message names the loss).
- **Material round-trip**: bury 7 stone -> dig -> 7 stone, message honest.
- **Dual wood ledgers**: `v.wood` (hearth woodpile) vs `stash.materials.wood`
  (communal building stock) are separate piles by design; takeWood's
  addWood entries carry `material:'wood'` so donate/take move them between
  piles without duplication — no double-spend path found. spendMaterial's
  `return true` on n<=0 is a latent footgun with a single clamped caller;
  left alone (minimal-change).
- **UI wiring**: bury-go / take confirmations gate on the engine result
  (no false "Buried."/"Drew from the cache." stamps); take-qty input
  negative values refused at engine level.

## Design calls made (Steve: "figure it out yourself")

1. Burial is a round-trip: the cache item keeps the FULL processing state
   (same contract as pantryAdd/takenStack) — no normalization, no loss.
2. Risk objects are copied on burial; pack leftovers and buried copies never
   alias.
3. `safe` is preserved as-is on burial (not normalized to true like the
   pantry) — the pantry normalizes because pantry food is "finished"; the
   earth keeps what you gave it.
