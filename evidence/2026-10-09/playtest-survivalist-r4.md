## 2 BREAKS found + fixed (src/js/game.js, src/js/food.js)

1. **Outdoor cooking burned zero fire fuel (E1).** `consumeCookFire` (food.js)
   filtered tracked fires on `f.burn0` — but only tent-interior fires ever set
   `burn0`; `makeFire`'s push omitted it. So every outdoor player fire slipped
   the filter: cooking outdoors paid only wall-clock ticks, never the 32-tick
   fuel session the code comments promise ("Per-item cooking honors fuel via
   consumeCookFire"), and the mid-cook death downgrade was unreachable outside
   tents. Proof: fire with 200 ticks of fuel, cookFood → 168 remaining pre-fix
   (wall clock only), 136 post-fix (32 fuel + 32 wall); a dying fire (20 ticks)
   cooked 800 kcal identical to a healthy fire pre-fix, 1400 vs 1600 post-fix
   (downgrade one outcome step, decent→undercooked). Fix: `makeFire` records
   `burn0` (initial fuel), and `consumeCookFire` drops the `burn0` filter
   (covers legacy saves whose outdoor fires lack it; map fires/hearths are
   untracked and stay free, as designed).
2. **Boiling burned zero fuel and ignored a dying fire (E2).** `boilWater`
   checked the fire at entry but never called `consumeCookFire` — a 32-tick
   boil cost the fire nothing, and a fire with 20 ticks left "boiled" 3L clean
   while dying under the pot, copy claiming "Boiled 3L… tending the fire."
   Fix: the boil burns 32 ticks of fuel like a cooking session; a fire that
   dies under the pot fails the batch honestly — water stays risky, copy says
   "The fire died under the pot — the water never came to a boil. Still risky.
   Feed the fire and try again." Work cost (kcal + 32 ticks) still charged.
   Moss-tinder boils unchanged (no fire to burn). Hearth boiling unchanged.

Design call (documented): a fire with exactly ≤32 ticks of fuel fails the
boil outright rather than partially — consistent with cooking's downgrade
fiction (one batch, one outcome), and the honest copy teaches the edge.
