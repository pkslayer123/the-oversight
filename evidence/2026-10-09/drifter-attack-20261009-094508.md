Hostile-player audit of the distance verbs: catch-up sim, join/leave,
absence accounting, village-card gates, mid-combat approach, home-village
absence, away deaths. Travel/map rounds 1–4 and the 2026-10-08 drifter runs
had already hardened fog, teleports, barriers, and the regrow watermark —

Proof test: `scripts/test-drifter-absence.js` (44 asserts, green x3 seeds:

## BREAK 1 — EXPLOIT/STATE: starvation deaths resurrected by the membership wrapper (REAL)

**Attack:** kill a villager via the famine path in `villageEats` while away.

**Measured (pre-fix, seeded):** health=1 villager + empty pantry + player at
(0,0) → endDay → famine death fires, `removeVillager` correctly drops the
roster 12→11… and by end of endDay the roster is 12 again WITH the corpse.
`membership.js` wraps `villageEats` to withhold non-members from the pot:
`var saved = v.roster` … `finally { v.roster = saved; }`. The `finally`
restored the pre-call array wholesale, resurrecting anyone who died
mid-call as a dead-marked zombie — back on the roster, eating again
tomorrow, counted in meals, while `vpOf().dead` says corpse.

**Fix (membership.js):** capture the pre-call non-members (`withheld`) up
front; the `finally` rebuilds as live-roster + withheld — deaths honored,
non-members restored, no zombies. Single save/restore site in the codebase;
no siblings.

**Proof:** D8 — corpse stays off the roster post-endDay; non-member
withholding still works (meals suite 20/20, exile-haven-reset 39/39 green).

## BREAK 2 — HONESTY: away-deaths announced in real time (REAL, 3 sites)

**Attack:** villagers die of starvation / thirst / sickness while the player
camps 50 miles out.

**Measured (pre-fix):** wound deaths queue to `scholar.awayNews` ("they'll
tell you when you're back" — the documented contract), but starvation
(`villageEats` famine), thirst (`villageDrinks`), and sickness
(`villageSicknessTick`) `say`'d immediately during endDay. The away player
got a real-time death bulletin with no vector — and the homecoming
"while you were gone" digest undercounted, because those deaths never
queued.

**Fix (game.js, 3 sites):** mirror the wound pattern — presence-gate the
say on `playerAtHaven()`; when away, push to `awayNews` (cap 8, same as
wounds). No new functions; ontology untouched (50/50).

**Proof:** D8 — all three death kinds: not said while away, queued to
awayNews, still said immediately at haven (control). The homecoming digest
now counts them.

## Design flags (not changed)

- A 300k fresh-food dump rots on day 4 (`spoilDay: day+3`) — the food-reality
  system working as designed. You can't prep-and-abandon on fresh forage;
  preserved stores are the honest way to leave (test stocks preserved).
- The home village scatters ~day 11 with zero player contribution — the
  designed scarcity pressure (village-as-protagonist), not a bug. Flagging
  for the food-economy run, not this one.
- `returnToVillage`'s "glad to see you" branch doesn't exclude `villageLost`
  — unreachable in real play (villageLost early-returns in endDay before any
  returnToVillage call). Latent copy edge, left alone.
- Sickness *recoveries* ("on the mend") still say in real time while away —
  deaths-only contract per the documented design; recoveries are a
  possible follow-up.

## Design calls made (Steve: "figure it out yourself")

1. Away deaths (starvation/thirst/sickness) queue to awayNews like wound
   deaths — no real-time death telepathy from 50 miles out.
2. The membership wrapper's finally honors mid-call deaths — dead stay dead;
   only pre-call non-members are restored.
