# Survival safety nets — Part C (2026-10-10)

Worker: survival-nets (oversight-survival-econ). Scope: earned safety nets for the
first ~30 days, as PLAYED SYSTEMS, not flat relief, not numbers tuning.
Diagnosis context: win-seeking policy, 60 seeds × 200 days — 0 wins, median
survival 24d vs ~100d target; villages lose ~12 members by day 24; 0/60 reach
Haven tier 1. Numbers siblings retune yields/lethality; this build keeps early
villages alive through systems.

## Design calls (for Steve's overrule)

1. **Struggle = signals, not a vibe.** `aidQuestStruggle()` reads: per-capita
   pantry < 2 days, recent death (≤3d), ≥2 sick, starving scholar. Aid quests
   and aid requests require genuine struggle — BUT a pantry-only "crisis" does
   not qualify alone (needs a body, a fever, or a starving scholar alongside).
   Anti-farm reasoning: dumping the pantry costs more kcal than any aid pays —
   the trigger is negative-EV to fake. Documented, not just asserted.
2. **The System's help is wrong in METHOD, never in VALUE.** 1-in-4 quests are
   bizarre (bring rocks / hug the sick / shout plant names) — canon: alien and
   out of touch. The reward is always real (real kcal, real ability choice).
   The System misunderstands; it doesn't shortchange.
3. **Relief is villager-driven, always.** Triage tents / rationing votes /
   emergency hunts are proposed by a NAMED villager (`_speaker` picks the
   steadiest living hands). The System never speaks in relief beats — so
   pre-day-7 villages get the identical path, per the brief.
4. **Rationing is real on both sides of the bowl.** Villagers: `need` halves via
   a chain-wrap on `villagerMealDay` (restores `kcalPerDay` after the call —
   verified). Player: the evening pantry meal draws half and the rest goes
   back into the bins as real pieces (verified: 1000→500 at trust 10). Morale
   goes to 'strained' + `leadShift('unity', -1)`. Cost named before the vote.
5. **Triage costs 800 kcal, honestly refused when short.** Sick `daysLeft` −2
   (min 1); wounded +25 health. Once per relief per option — "we don't get to
   spend that twice."
6. **Emergency hunts name the hunters and can hurt them.** 1500–4000 kcal real
   meat via `stockPantry`; 25% injury each (10–20 dmg through `hurtVillager`,
   the real pipeline). Once per relief.
7. **Neighbor aid moves REAL kcal between REAL pantries.** Theirs is the
   abstract `pantryKcal` number (other villages are simmed); ours is physical
   pieces. Accepted food aid: their −2000, ours +2000 via `stockPantry`. They
   can run dry and refuse honestly ("their pantry is honestly bare: ~N kcal").
8. **The request move is knowledge-gated.** `state.aidKnown` — learned via
   trader gossip, an inbound offer, or watching a beg. The Haven panel only
   renders "Ask for aid" buttons once known. Knowledge never gates RELIEF
   (camera rule from comms.js holds).
9. **Standing bar matches the call-for-help chain: trust ≥ 35**, said aloud
   when refused. Credit is real: `link.aidCreditKcal` (+10 ask score later);
   ingratitude (refusing a creditor after taking their food) costs −10 trust
   and travels as gossip.
10. **Finite everywhere:** quests max 3/run, 4-day cooldown, days 7–21 only;
    requests 1/village/week + only while struggling; offers 5-day global
    cooldown + only while struggling; neighbor pantries finite; relief options
    once per crisis; relief expires in ~3 days of parts.
11. **Bug fixed along the way (shared code):** `aidFaceFor` (comms.js) stored
    the raw `{name, cultureId}` object from `genNameForOrigin` as the face's
    name for speaker-less villages → "[object Object]" in beats. Unwrapped at
    the source; my module also unwraps defensively (`_faceName`).

## Proof

`scripts/test-survival-nets.js` — 100 checks × 3 seeds (777010, 424242, 9001),
all green:
- Net 1 (quests): no offer pre-day-7 / not-struggling / cooldown / budget;
  fetch played end-to-end (meat really left the pack, supply really landed);
  treat completes on recovery, fails on death; learn completes on +2 plants;
  deadline expiry = real failure (integration −2); weird variants checkable;
  ability reward → the System's choice sheet (slot-capped).
- Net 2 (relief): cascade raises relief via `villageSicknessTick`, pre-day-7
  identical, System silent; triage honest cost + refused when short; rationing
  halves villager need AND player meal (both verified numerically); hunt stocks
  real meat; expiry; raid/storm/hunger hooks; one-relief-at-a-time; dismiss.
- Net 3 (neighbors): knowledge gate; gossip teaches; request → runner → aloud
  decision → real delivery both directions (their pantry thinned); cooldown;
  bare-neighbor honest refusal (−2 trust); trust gate; no-ask-when-fed;
  inbound offers (rich neighbor, teaches the move); offer refuse (−3 trust);
  beg give (real kcal both ways, +4 trust, credit); ingratitude (−10, ledgered,
  gossiped); bare-bins honest no; medicine cures; hands crew 3 days then leaves.
- Exploit: 30 days of maximum aggression — neighbor pantry visibly depletes
  (22000→~10000, refuses when bare), total received bounded (≤16000), quest
  budget held (≤3), cooldowns held.
- Reachability: every `data-beat`/`data-aidreq` maps to an existing engine fn;
  beats-row renders every open beat.
- Live-path check: real `endDay()` fired the quest offer; real `advancePart()`
  fired the part tick.
- Regressions: `test-aid-comms-20261010.js` 71/71 (closest suite; covers the
  shared `aidFaceFor` change); ontology 58/58.
