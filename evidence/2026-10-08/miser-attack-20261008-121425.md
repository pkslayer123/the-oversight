## EXPLOIT — broke + fixed (4)

**E1. Stash trust farm: donate-10 / take-9 (REAL, measured +20 trust / 20 cycles, 1 unit/cycle).**
The miser loop's take-back sting only fired at net<=0, so parking net at +1
printed +1 trust per cycle forever. Fix (design call, "figure it out
yourself" authority): **trust follows NET contribution per material in
10-unit bands** — crossing a band upward grants, withdrawing back across
revokes band-for-band. Bands telescope: total trust from donations =
floor(finalNet/10) - floor(initialNet/10) no matter how you cycle, so no
donate/take rhythm can beat the straight-donation rate. The full take-back
sting (-5, mirroring the pantry rule) is the whole consequence when it
fires — no double-punishment. Ledgers are now per-material
(`v.stashGives[vid]` = `{branch: n, ...}`; legacy flat numbers migrate to
`__legacy`). Post-fix: 20 cycles net +0 trust (15->17 band oscillation).

**E2. Exiled stash looting (REAL, measured 5 wood taken, no refusal).**
Exile doesn't move you; `enterBuilding()` has no membership check, so an
exiled player walks back into the old hall and `takeMaterial`/`takeTool`
work freely while the pantry stays closed. Fix: `takeMaterial` and
`takeTool` added to `_blockIfExiled` (membership.js) — "The stash is not
yours anymore." DONATIONS stay allowed: gifts toward amends are how the
exiled earn trust back, and the justice loop's "convince the village" path
needs a verb.

**E3. Cache-theft evasion via village fork (REAL, robber = null).**
`dailyCacheCheck` rolled only against the CURRENT village + otherVillages.
After fork/join the old village is archived to `pastVillages`: caches near
old Haven were measured at the wrong (far) distance AND the robber pool was
the founder-only roster (→ null robber, no trace ever). Fix: spots include
`pastVillages` carrying their village ref; `pickCacheRobber(village?)`,
`resolveCacheRobbery(c, village?)`, `plantCacheTheftSuspicion(vid, c,
village?)` thread the NEAREST village through, so old-village caches are
robbed by old villagers at the honest distance. Post-fix: robber ∈ {v2, v3}
(old roster), never null.

**E4. takeFromCache NaN-unit infinite (REAL, measured 3 takes → 3 units from a unit-less item).**
A cache item with missing/NaN units went `it.units -= qty` → NaN, survived
every take, and yielded 1 unit per take FOREVER. Fix: coerce once at take
(`it.units = Math.max(1, Math.floor(it.units || 1))`) — corrupt entries
collapse to exactly one honest unit, never an infinite.

## HONESTY — broke + fixed (3)

**H1. False take-back accusation, materials (REAL).** Donate 10 branch, take
10 stone → "You took back what you gave. They noticed. Trust -5." — false:
communal sharing, not a take-back. Fix: per-material ledgers; the sting
fires only for the SAME material you gave with net≤0.

**H2. False take-back accusation, tools (REAL).** Donate axe, take saw →
"You took back the tool you left." — false. Fix: `stashToolGives/Takes`
per itemId; sting only when you take a tool you left and haven't re-taken.
True positive preserved (re-taking your own axe still stings, -5).

**H3. Bury-go confirmation lied (REAL).** The caches UI stamped "Buried."
even when `buryCache` refused (mirrors the takeFromCache wiring which
already gated on res). Fix: `result: res ? 'Buried.' : null`.

## HELD (attacked, resisted)

- **Spoil double-spend**: `sweepSpoiled` covers pack/prep/pantry/corpses but
  NEVER caches (rot is lazy at dig/take). A buried item can't be swept and
  drawn — verified A7: spoiled buried berries unrecoverable.
- **Cache+pantry+pack triple-registration**: bury removes from pack, dig
  removes from cache, `stockPantry` mints fresh items. No path registers
  one item in two containers.
- **Infinite stash / hoard without cost**: no capacity cap is by design
  (communal pile); the cost mechanism is the ledger + trust system, and
  closed villages skim. Materials are real throughout — no phantom.
- **Steal-from-self / cache-theft punishment evasion**: `pickCacheRobber`
  excludes the player; distance-based rates are the designed miser promise
  (far = safer). Held.
- **Caches across player death**: the run wipes on death ("Dead runs are
  wiped") — buried goods die with the run. Honest: the mantle passes, the
  body doesn't.
- **Caches across fork/join**: kept deliberately — they're in the WORLD,
  not the village ("What crosses the fire: yourself, Codex, pack" is about
  the village reset; the world persists). E3 keeps the theft sim honest
  about them.
- **Double-withdraw (pantry/stash)**: decrement-then-splice everywhere;
  bulk-pack index-stability already hardened by the food loop.
- **Tool donate/take trust farm**: donate +2 / re-take -5 = net -3/cycle.
  Held (never farmable).

## Design calls made (Steve: "figure it out yourself")

1. Trust follows net contribution in 10-unit bands (telescoping, farm-proof
   by construction). Small gifts accumulate into bands — 10×1 branch = +1,
   same rate as bulk; nothing to farm.
2. Full take-back = exactly -5 (sting is the whole consequence, mirrors
   pantry); partial withdrawals unwind band-for-band.
3. Exiled players may DONATE to the old stash (amends path) but not TAKE.
4. Caches survive fork/join (world-persistent) but stay robbable by whoever
   lives nearest — including archived villages.

## FUN notes

- Delights: the ledger as a social object ("The ledger says everything")
  keeps landing — taking from the stash FEELS watched, which is the whole
  miser fantasy. The ration-drawer (partial cache takes) is the miser verb
  done right: drawing 2 days' food from a winter stockpile without digging
  it up feels clever, not fiddly.
- Drags: the stash UI's "Take 5" fixed quantum fights the band math a
  little (take 5 of a 10-donation unwinds nothing until the second take) —
  acceptable, the copy never promises per-unit trust. The bury form's
  food-index options could go stale if inventory changes mid-render, but
  the form re-renders on every refresh, so the window is nil.
- The fork-theft fix quietly improves the fiction: your old village's
  hungry mouths remember your hiding spots. That's a story, not a mechanic.
