## What broke (3 catches, all fixed)

### 1. DEAD — group encounters never wired (the Alien Players lesson, again)
`apRollGroupEncounter` / `apStartGroupEncounter` / `apGroupBanter` were built,
documented in the @ontology header ("rare late-game 2-3 persona team encounters
(day 40+, 3%, 14-day cooldown, needs 2+ established rivals)"), and called from
NOWHERE. Before-proof: spied both across 300 eligible `checkEncounter` rolls —
36 single encounters fired, **0 group calls**. Worse, `apStartGroupEncounter`
wrote `state.alienGroup` ("The others are circling. They'll join the fight in
turn.") and nothing ever read it — the promise was a lie.
Fix: (a) `encounters.js` checkEncounter wrap now rolls `apRollGroupEncounter()`
first, falls back to the single pool; (b) the `tbEnd` wrap chains the next
persona via `state.alienGroup` when a group fight is WON — old fight is over
(`tbfight.over=true`), so `startAlienCombat`'s inCombat guard passes and the new
fight replaces the corpse. Fleeing or losing disperses the group (no ambush on a
retreat). `apStartGroupEncounter` cleans up `state.alienGroup` if the first
fight fails to start.
After-proof: forced roll returns a 2-group, banter fires, fight 1 starts,
`tbEnd('won')` chains fight 2 (countess_sable steps in, announced), second
`tbEnd('won')` ends cleanly with no phantom third fighter; `tbEnd('fled')`
disperses with no chain. 7 group starts in 300 rolls (~2.3%, matches 3% design).
Ontology (groups) rule updated.

### 2. EXPLOIT — same-rival sporting rule bypassed by the weighted pool
`apRollEncounter`: the due-rival path enforced "min 2 days between hunts by the
same persona", but the weighted fallback pool did NOT filter on `lastHuntDay` —
a hostile player tile-bouncing could re-fight yesterday's sadistic rival daily.
Before-proof: vex (fought yesterday) re-picked **400/400** forced rolls.
Fix: weighted pool now skips personas with `day - lastHuntDay < 2`; fallback to
the unfiltered pool only if the filter empties it (fought the whole roster in 2
days — extreme). After-proof: **0/400** re-picks, rolls still succeed (400/400
via fallback).

### 3. DEAD — `apBeamResistText` never called
Player-facing beam-readiness readout ("The player should KNOW when they're not
ready") with zero call sites. Before-proof: 0 calls across combat intro, beam
hit, and 30 daily ticks.
Fix: `apStartEncounter` now says it once per player, gated on
`apKnowsAlien(pid)` (pre-reveal it would name the alien truth; the horror beat
covers the unready) and `apHasBeam(pid)` — new helper, single source of truth
for "fields a beam weapon" (everyone but Old Tam), also referenced by
`apMaybeBeamAttack`. After-proof: fires once when known, silent pre-reveal,
silent for beamless Old Tam, never repeats.

Also: `apPilotTaunt` and `apHasBeam` added to the @ontology provides list
(validator requires provides↔definitions match); `docs/ONTOLOGY.md`
regenerated by the validator. Validator: "All 47 systems validated".
