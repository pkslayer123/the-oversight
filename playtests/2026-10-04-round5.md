## 🐛 Real bug: T2b — talking destroys high trust

**Repro (end-to-end):**
1. `giveFood(vid)` ×5 → NPC trust = 75
2. `talkTo(vid)` once → NPC trust = **40**

**Root cause** (`src/js/game.js`, `talkTo`):
```js
const newTrust = Math.min(40, trust + 3 * dipMult);
```
This doesn't cap *gains* at 40 — it *sets* trust to `min(40, ...)`, dragging any higher trust down to 40. Talking to a friend you earned trust with (via food gifts, which raise individual NPC trust to 100) makes them trust you less.

**Fix (one line):** only apply the cap when trust is below it:
```js
const newTrust = trust >= 40 ? trust : Math.min(40, trust + 3 * dipMult);
```

**Impact in practice:** rare but real. Individual NPC trust above 40 currently comes only from `giveFood` (+12/meal, cap 100). A player who feeds an NPC then chats with them gets punished. The 25 playthroughs recorded 0 in-the-wild hits because bots donate to the pantry (village trust, keyed by scholar id) rather than giving food to individuals — but any human who shares meals will hit it.

Note: the build attestation claimed this path "caps only conversation-earned trust at 40 without reducing higher trust." The source does reduce higher trust. The attestation was wrong.

## Time-economy fixes confirmed in live play

- Talkers now spend 20 kcal/conversation — they can no longer talk forever. (In the v1 bot, talkers stalled on day 1 burning 68 free talks; with the 20-kcal cost they must interleave foraging, and all 4 talker runs survived to day 13.)
- The donate/take-back and boil-water bypasses are closed (verified mechanically in Phase 1; no bot found a way around them).
- Water hauling costs are real: bots fill 1L at a time at 10 kcal/L.

## Harness notes (bot flaws, not game bugs)

1. **v1 bot stalled:** free actions (talkTo, donateToPantry, huntAnimal, cookAll) don't advance the day-part — correct per the action economy (time / time+calories / calories-only) — but the v1 bot treated each call as a "part" and burned its 68-part budget on day 1. Rewrote as v2: free actions + exactly one time-advancing action per part.
2. **`travelTo` doesn't advance time** (calories-only: 30 kcal/tile). Travel-heavy styles (scavenger walking to ruins) consume parts without advancing days. Bumped the part cap 72 → 168 for clean 12-day completions. This is intended design, but worth noting: a player can cross the whole map in one day-part if they can afford the calories.
3. **Ruin fog-of-war:** ruins spawn within d≤3 of haven but unrevealed; `travelTargets()` only lists revealed tiles or adjacent fog. Bots must step toward known coordinates. Correct game behavior (round 3 verified greedy walk reaches it).
4. **Cook needs pantry staples:** foraged food has no `rawKcal`; the cook bot takes beans/rice from the pantry then `cookAll`s at haven. Pipeline verified working (150 raw → 300 cooked).
5. All 25 bots chose green_thumb — see above. Future rounds should weight style actions harder if ability-choice variety is under test.
