Even an **optimal survivalist bot** (eats properly, cooks, manages water, forages efficiently) dies by day 5 with -5,000 to -7,000 kcal.

**The game is mathematically unsustainable.**

## Root cause: action economy

The math:
- Daily burn: 2,200 kcal (resolveDay) + action costs (forage 120, travel 320/leg, etc.)
- Forage yield: ~760 kcal average (tested across 10 tiles)
- Forages needed: ~3/day just to break even
- Day parts: 4 (so 4 actions max)
- Actions needed: travel (1) + forage (3) + cook (1) + eat (1) + water (1) = 7

**You need 7 actions but get 4.** Even perfect play can't cover the deficit. Once you fall behind, the spiral is irreversible.

## What's working

- **Village pantry sustainable** — after fixing villageEats to use real items and making villagers self-sufficient, pantry grows (34k → 46k). The village doesn't starve itself anymore.
- **Cooking system** — fire at Haven works, water cost enforced, raw→cooked doubles calories, unsafe→safe.
- **Water bottles** — 1L/1kg, clean vs risky, boil at fire, 30% sickness chance for risky. Weight matters.
- **Equipment slots** — no more "best in backpack." Equip one weapon, one armor. Clean UI.
- **Multi-save** — one slot per character, title screen lists them.
- **Pathfinding** — BFS works, avoids blocked cells, cost displayed.
- **Eat preserves gear** — finally.

## Game-breaking issues

### 1. Survival impossible (CRITICAL)
Even optimal play dies by day 5. The action economy doesn't work. Either:
- Forage yields need to be 2-3x higher, OR
- Daily need needs to be lower (1500?), OR  
- More actions per day (6-8 parts?), OR
- Foraging needs to be more efficient (one forage = one day's food)

Steve's design says "food is scarcity" but this is "food is impossible."

### 2. Village trust trap
Villagers only share 20% of surplus at trust < 30. Trust builds via talking, which takes actions you don't have (see #1). The village is sustainable now, but the player can't benefit from it early.

### 3. Cooking water cost is brutal
5 beans = 5L water. Village starts with 20L. That's 4 cooking sessions. Then you need to refill (travel to creek, fill 1L at a time). Each fill is an action. The water economy compounds the action crisis.

## Serious issues

### 4. Mara's bot looped searching the same room
The room search didn't mark as searched properly, or the bot couldn't tell. It searched (1,1) repeatedly. Either the `searched` flag isn't set, or the UI doesn't communicate it.

### 5. Negative kcal allowed
`resolveDay` lets kcal go to -8,000. Should floor at 0 (you're dead at 0, not -8000). The health spiral works, but the number is absurd.

### 6. Jesse banked food then starved
He moved ALL food to pantry, then had nothing to eat. The "bank surplus" logic needs to keep a personal reserve. (Bot issue, but a human might do this too — the UI should warn.)

## Clunky / poor mechanics

### 7. Take from pantry one at a time
`takeFromPantry(0)` takes ONE unit. To get 2000 kcal of beans (13 units), you tap 13 times. Needs "take 5" or "take 10" or "take until X kcal."

### 8. Fill water one at a time
Same — 1L per action. To fill 5 bottles, 5 actions. That's more than a full day.

### 9. No bulk cook
`cookAll()` cooks everything, but if you only want to cook 5 of your 20 beans, you can't. All-or-nothing.

### 10. Eat is all-or-nothing
`eat()` eats until 2400 or out of food. Can't eat "just a snack" (500 kcal). Sometimes you want to conserve.

## Missing concepts

### 11. No way to preserve food
Smoking, drying, salting — real survival techniques. Food spoils in 3 days, but you can't do anything about it.

### 12. No fishing
Creeks have water but you can't fish. Real survival staple.

### 13. Traps don't work
`setTrap` exists, but the critic couldn't get it to produce food. The recipe needs vine + stone, but vine is hard to find.

## What I'd prioritize

1. **Fix the action economy** — this is existential. The game can't ship if you die by day 5 playing perfectly.
2. **Bulk actions** — take 10, fill 5, cook 5. The one-at-a-time is tedious.
3. **Floor kcal at 0** — negative 8000 is silly.
4. **Room search flag** — make sure searched rooms stay searched.

## Verdict

The systems are well-designed and the fixes work. But the game isn't playable yet — not because of bugs, but because the numbers don't add up. It's a balance problem, not a code problem.

The good news: it's a *tunable* balance problem. Increase forage yields, decrease daily need, or give more actions. Any of those fixes it. But it needs to be fixed before playtesting continues — right now we're just watching bots starve.
