# Day-1 Playtest as Steve — 2026-10-04

Played like Steve: fresh start, Texas origin, day 1 dawn, poking at everything. Drove the real engine via Node harness (`/tmp/steve-day1.js`, `steve-day1b.js`, `pathcheck.js`, `travelcheck.js`, `pantrycheck.js`, `questcheck.js`). Picky on purpose.

## CRITICAL

### 1. WATER bar shows `[object Object]` — visible in the first 10 seconds
`statusBars()` in app.js does `st.hydration + '% · ' + st.water + ' clean'`, but `st.water` is an array of bottle objects. Renders literally as:

> **100% · [object Object],[object Object] clean**

Same bug class as the ability-cost `[object Object]` from yesterday. Steve sees the stat bars before anything else. Fix: sum liters, e.g. `2L clean`.

### 2. Your intro quest is given by someone who doesn't exist
The wake-up speech ("You're awake. Don't sit up fast…") comes from a fixed main (Mara/Jesse/Aki/Priya/Theo/Ruth). None of them are in the village roster — all 12 villagers are generated/background characters. Verified: giver `priya_nair` is not in `state.village.roster`, has no grid position. She wakes you up, gives the speech, vanishes forever. Day-1 fiction break on the first real screen. Fix: give the quest lines to a generated character who's actually there, or put one fixed main in every roster.

### 3. PACK bar lies about weight (excludes water)
Pantry summary says "6.5 kg" after packing food + 3L water. The PACK stat bar says "4.5/20 kg". `packWeight()` only sums inventory — `waterWeight()` is excluded. The bulk-take capacity check *does* include water, so the game knows, but the bar doesn't show it. Player can silently overpack water. Fix: include `waterWeight()` in `packWeight()`.

## HIGH

### 4. "Talk" pill has no name — the strip is noise, not signal
The context strip shows a bare **Talk** pill whenever any villager is within 3 tiles. No name. Worse, `cellActions()` checks distance from the *player*, not the *cell* — so every one of the 9 scanned cells reports "Talk" if anyone's in earshot (deduped to one pill in the real UI, but still context-free). Steve's rule: actions should appear *for the thing you're next to*. Fix: pill should read "Talk — [Name]" for the nearest villager.

### 5. The grounds aren't standardized — only the hall is
Steve asked for one haven layout so new players learn it once. The hall is fixed. But `exitBuilding()` → the grounds are **randomly generated every run** (tents, fires, dirt, grass via seeded RNG). Tents and fires both block movement, and the RNG can build mazes — in one seed the player had to thread around 6+ blockers to reach an edge. The "familiar home base" promise breaks the moment you step outside. Fix: standardize the grounds layout too (fixed tent/fire/lodge positions, guaranteed clear paths to all four edges).

### 6. The lodge is walkable — you can stroll through the building
`cellProps('lodge').blocks` is `undefined` (falsy = walkable). The player emerges at (4,2) south of the lodge and can walk straight through its walls. Fix: lodge blocks, except the doorstep cell.

### 7. Fire pits block movement
🔥 is an impassable tile. On the random grounds, fires spawn at 10% and can seal paths. A fire pit blocking a grown adult is silly — you can step around a campfire. Fix: fire shouldn't block (or at most costs a little care to step adjacent).

### 8. "No path there" is a dead end, not guidance
Tapping a blocked edge cell (fire, tent) → `movePath` fails → "No path there." The player was told "walk to the edge, tap yourself, head out" — they did, and it didn't work, with no hint to try a different edge cell. Fix: on failure, say *why* ("A fire pit blocks the way — try another edge") or auto-suggest the nearest walkable edge cell.

## MEDIUM

### 9. Zero first-time guidance after the wake-up
After "Open your eyes" → quest speech → you're standing in the hall. Nothing tells you to step outside, pack food, or drink water. The dayPartHint ("The world wakes. Animals are active.") is flavor, not instruction. The new-player critic flagged this too. Steve hasn't asked for a tutorial, but *something* — even one line in the quest speech ("Pack food from the pantry before you go") — would cut the flailing.

### 10. Corner edgeExit always picks W/E, never N/S
At (0,0), `edgeExit` checks `cx === 0` first → always west. The comment claims "corners pick the axis you tapped last" but the code doesn't. Minor, but the travel direction can surprise.

### 11. `travelTo` returns `undefined` on success, `{kind:'blockage'}` on failure
Inconsistent API. The UI handles it, but anything else calling `travelTo` has to guess. Return `{kind:'ok'}` on success.

## LOW

### 12. Haven panel "mains" list is always empty
`roster.filter(r => r.isMain)` — nothing sets `isMain` on generated/background characters, so every villager lands under "Also here:". Either wire `isMain` or drop the split.

## What's actually good (for balance)
- Wake-up reveal text is strong. "You don't remember choosing this. You didn't." lands.
- Pantry slider UI works end-to-end: pack, merge, water-independent, Mcal formatting ("4.8 Mcal, 6.5 kg") reads clean on a phone.
- Standardized hall interior is legible: walls ⬛, doors 🚪, bunks 🛏️, fire 🔥 — you can read it at a glance.
- Edge-walk → tap-self → "Head east" travel flow works when the edge cell is walkable. Fog-of-war guesses on the minimap are intact.
- Quest intro writing (Mara/Jesse/etc.) is good — it just needs to come from someone who's actually there.
