# Balance Stress Test — 2026-10-04

**10 playthroughs, 10 days each, 4 min-max policies.** Numbers, not vibes.

## Test Setup

| Policy | N | Behavior |
|--------|---|----------|
| Forager | 3 | Forage whenever possible, BFS to plants, bank surplus, eat when low |
| Hunter | 3 | Prioritize `huntAnimal()`, chase animals, forage only as backup |
| Social | 2 | Stay in haven, talk to everyone, eat only when kcal < 500 |
| Rusher | 2 | Minimal survival to day 7, then choose + spam abilities |

All runs: `newGame('Ohio', 'creek_bottom', null, [])`, 10-day cap, 4 day-parts/day.

### Q1: Which strategy wins? By how much?

**Forager wins, but it's not a blowout — it's the only one that doesn't die.**

- Forager #1: 9 days, health 63, 2280 kcal in reserve. The only run that looks healthy.
- Forager #2/#3: 8 days, health 26, 0 kcal. Survived but barely. (Bot got stuck — see caveats.)
- Hunter: All runs starving (health 13-23, 0 kcal). 0-3 successful hunts in 8-9 days.
- Social: Dead (health 0). 32 talks each, trust 300+. Died with friends.
- Rusher: Survived to day 7, got 1 ability each, used it 3-7 times. Health 62-65. Viable but thin.

**The gap:** Forager #1 ended with 2280 kcal banked. Everyone else ended at 0. That's not a margin, that's a different game.

### Q2: Is there a dominant strategy that makes others pointless?

**Yes. Foraging is the only viable primary food source. This is a problem.**

**Hunting math:**
| Animal | Kcal | Difficulty | Success | EV/hunt | Cost | Net EV |
|--------|------|------------|---------|---------|------|--------|
| Rabbit | 800 | easy | 70% | 560 | 240 | **+320** |
| Squirrel | 350 | medium | 40% | 140 | 240 | **-100** |
| Opossum | 1200 | easy | 70% | 840 | 240 | **+600** |
| Turkey | 3000 | medium | 40% | 1200 | 240 | **+960** |
| Deer | 20000 | hard | 15% | 3000 | 240 | **+2760** |

On paper, hunting has positive EV for most animals. In practice:
- Animals spawn on only ~25% of tile entries
- They flee when you approach (1 cell/turn)
- They despawn at grid edges
- Hunter bot managed 0-3 hunts in 8-9 days

**Theoretical EV is irrelevant if you can't find the animal.** Hunting is a lottery ticket, not a strategy.

**Social is suicide.** 32 talks = 640 kcal spent on conversation (20 kcal/talk), zero food gained. Trust 300+ is meaningless if you're dead. The design intends social to be valuable (villagers share food at high trust), but you don't live long enough to collect.

**Rusher is a trap.** You must survive 7 days with no powers to get powers. By day 7 you're already committed to foraging to stay alive. The ability is a bonus, not a strategy.

### Q3: Are metabolic costs actually constraining, or can you ignore them?

**They're brutally constraining. You cannot ignore them.**

From `src/js/engine/calories.js`:
- Base burn: **2200 kcal/day** (ACTIVE_DAY)
- BMR: 1800 kcal/day at rest
- Forage: 120 kcal/action
- Hunt: 240 kcal/action
- Travel: 320 kcal/leg
- System ability: 800 kcal/use
- Starvation threshold: 500 kcal reserve

**Daily budget for an active forager:**
- Base: 2200
- 4 forages: 480
- 1 travel: 320
- **Total: ~3000 kcal/day**

**Income needed:** ~3000 kcal/day just to break even.

**Forage yields (measured):** ~193 kcal/forage average, huge variance (70-350).

**The math:** 3000 / 193 = **15.5 forages/day to break even.** You have 4 day-parts. Even foraging every part, you get 4 forages = ~772 kcal. **You're running a 2200 kcal/day deficit.**

**How does anyone survive?** Starting supplies. Character creation gives 5 items. Village starts with 36,000 kcal (1.5 days for 12 people). You're burning through the buffer.

**This is the intended design** (Steve: "food is a real scarcity"), but the numbers say: **you cannot forage your way to sustainability.** The deficit is structural, not skill-based.

### Q4: Do synergies change the balance meaningfully?

**No. Zero synergies discovered in 10 days across all runs.**

The new active-discovery system requires 3 successful combined uses. In 10 days:
- Rushers got 1 ability each (day 7+)
- You need 2+ abilities to even attempt a synergy
- 3 days isn't enough to discover, let alone exploit

**Synergies are irrelevant to the 10-day balance.** They're a 20+ day feature. That's fine — they're meant to be late-game depth — but they don't affect the survival calculus.

### Q5: Is the game too easy? Too hard? Where's the sweet spot?

**Too hard in the wrong way.**

The survival pressure is real and intended. But:

1. **The calorie math doesn't work.** You can't forage enough to survive. The game is a countdown timer, not a strategy space.

2. **Hunting is broken as a primary source.** Not because the numbers are wrong, but because the encounter rate is too low. 25% spawn on tile entry, plus flee mechanics, means you can't rely on it.

3. **Social is a death sentence.** The trust→food-sharing pipeline exists, but you starve before it pays off.

4. **The only viable play is:** forage + eat starting supplies + hope. That's not a strategy, that's a timer.

**Sweet spot would be:**
- Forage yields need to be ~2x higher, OR
- Daily burn needs to be ~1500 not 2200, OR  
- Hunting encounter rate needs to be 2-3x higher to be a real alternative

**Right now:** The game says "be smart about food" but the math says "you're dying no matter what, just slower if you forage."

## Critical Findings

### 🔴 The calorie economy is structurally deficit

You cannot eat enough to survive through gameplay. Starting supplies mask this for ~5 days, then the spiral hits. This isn't difficulty, it's a timer.

**Recommendation:** Either double forage yields or cut base metabolic to 1500. The current numbers don't support the "skilled forager thrives" fantasy.

### 🔴 Hunting is non-viable

25% spawn rate + flee + despawn = 0-3 hunts per 10 days. Even with positive EV per hunt, the encounter rate kills it.

**Recommendation:** Guarantee at least 1 animal per 2-3 tile entries, or add passive traps that work while you do other things.

### 🟡 Social has no survival path

Trust 300+ is achievable but you're dead. The villager food-sharing needs to kick in faster, or social needs a way to get food (begging? trading?).

### 🟢 Ability rush is correctly gated

Day 7 arrival + survival requirement is good design. Rushers survive but don't dominate. The 800 kcal/ability cost is meaningful.

### 🟢 Metabolic costs are felt

Nobody ignores them. Every decision has a calorie price. This is working as intended.

## Bot Caveats

- Forager #2/#3 got stuck (0 forages) — likely a pathfinding issue in the test harness, not the game. Forager #1 shows the policy works when the bot doesn't get stuck.
- Pantry donations weren't measured (test bug reading `pantryKcal` vs `pantry[]` array).
- Social bot didn't eat from village pantry (didn't test the sharing mechanic).
- 10 days is short for synergy/ability testing. Those are 20+ day features.
