# Live playtest — Muse as player, 2026-10-03

Playing via the engine with human judgment (can't test phone rendering/taps from here).
Goal: feel the loop, find friction.

## Setup
New game, Ohio, Mara Okafor. Quest plays (6 lines, good voice).
Standing on Haven 🏠. Panel shows 6 villagers, pantry 2000 (2.5 days), Journal (0).
Win condition visible: Codex 8+, Pantry 5000+.

FRICTION 1: I see "tap a highlighted tile to travel" but I don't know which tiles are good yet.
Everything is fog except Haven. That's correct (discovery), but my first move is a guess.
The breadbasket grove is adjacent — but I can't tell from the minimap (it's fog? or revealed?).
Actually reveal(3,3) reveals within distance 2, so I can see it. Good.

## Day 1
Dawn at Haven. I tap the adjacent grove (breadbasket). Travel: 1 part, 30 kcal.
Detail grid: trees, bushes, plants (🌱 — undiscovered). Feels like a place!
I forage (1 part, 120 kcal). Got dandelion (RNG, not hickory — discovery!).
Journal notes it. Tile is now "dandelion country" (my first find).

FRICTION 2: The tile says "dandelion country" but I can SEE it's a grove full of trees.
My journal records what I found, not what's there. That's honest, but feels odd
when the visual screams "nut trees." Maybe the label should be tentative:
"dandelion (so far)"? Or the visual plants should match the ecology more?

I forage again (stock 3→2→1). Got dandelion again (3x focus bonus working).
Third forage: hickory! (4x ecology beat the 3x focus). Journal: hickory.
But the tile label stays "dandelion country" (first find sticks).

FRICTION 3: I found hickory — the BETTER food — but the label doesn't update.
The map lies to me now. The label should reflect the best/highest-value discovery,
or the most recent, or list all. "Dandelion country" when I'm eating hickory feels wrong.

I eat to full (free action). Pack has surplus.
I walk home (tap Haven). Deposit: +~2000 kcal. Pantry 2000→~2800 (after 800 drain).
Villagers react. Good loop!

## Day 2
Back to the grove (stock regrew overnight). Forage 3x. Mostly hickory now.
I notice the detail grid plants show 🌰 (discovered!) instead of 🌱. Nice touch.
Eat, go home, deposit. Pantry climbing.

FRICTION 4: This is already feeling routine on day 2. The breadbasket grove is
TOO good and TOO close. There's no reason to explore. The "go find food" tension
is gone because home has infinite hickory. The breadbasket guarantees survival
but kills adventure. Maybe the breadbasket should be GOOD but not BEST?
Or its stock should be lower (2 instead of 3)?

## Day 3
I force myself to explore. Travel 2 tiles to a wetland. Detail: water pools, cattails (🌱).
Forage: cattail! New entry. Journal (3).
The edge blending works — I can see the grove's trees on the west edge. Continuous!

FRICTION 5: Travel cost is 1 part whether I go 1 tile or 3. So there's no reason
not to go 3 tiles. The "30 kcal/tile" is trivial. Distance doesn't matter,
only parts. This makes the map feel small. Should far travel cost more parts?
Or is the simplicity worth it?

## Day 4-5
I'm in a rhythm: grove 3x, home, deposit. Pantry 4000+. Codex 5.
The 4th entry triggered the CODEX ceremony. Nice moment!

FRICTION 6: I have 5 codex entries but I don't feel like I'm "discovering."
I'm just foraging the same grove. The discovery mechanic (first find labels tile)
isn't driving exploration because the breadbasket is sufficient. The WIN needs
8 entries, which forces me out — good. But the journey there feels like grinding
the same tile.

## Overall
The loop WORKS. Forage → eat → deposit → pantry climbs. The detail grid feels
like a world. Discovery is honest. The Codex ceremony is a good beat.
But: the breadbasket is too comfortable (kills exploration), the tile labels
lie after better discoveries, and travel distance is meaningless.

Balance: The sim said 23% win for a dumb bot. As a thinking player, I felt
in control by day 2. Might be slightly too easy once you understand it.
But "understand it" is the game — that's fine for slice 1.
