# Playtest — explorer loop (2026-10-04)

**Character**: explorer archetype (scheduled loop, rotation 1). Engine-driven play via node: depart → 6-hop travel loop through fog → examine sweeps on wilderness tiles → curiosity-hint verification.

## What I did
Traveled 6 nodes (meadow/creek/forest_floor/thicket): costs exactly 30 kcal/tile + 32 ticks/node, honest every hop; one creek blockage offered swim (paid the UI's 20 kcal) and cleared. Examined fresh cells across 3 wilderness tiles; measured feature density; stood adjacent to hidden features to test proximity hints.

## What I found (fixed this run)

1. **Discovery ran blind — no way to know a cell hides something.** The world seeds ~13 hidden features per wilderness tile (tracks/old camps/strange growths/hollows/remnants at 5–22% per eligible cell, measured 13.6–18.2% of fresh cells) but nothing signaled them: examining was a blind 2-tick lottery across 81 cells. Fix: `perceive.js` now surfaces one low-priority curiosity whisper ("The ground here looks disturbed. Worth a closer look.") when an unrevealed feature is adjacent — a hint, never a label. Still have to examine the exact cell to learn what it is; whisper goes quiet once revealed. Verified: hint present → examine reveals the story → hint gone.
2. **Haven interior cells were dead to examine.** `hall`/`bunk` fell through to "It declines to be interesting." Fix: home gets texture (whose mug is whose, blanket-folding as character reading). (In carexplore.js working tree — sibling has an in-flight hunk in the same file, so NOT committed this run; will land with their commit or a follow-up.)

## Feel verdict
The travel loop is the game's strongest system right now: fog-of-war reveals a radius-2 diamond, walking into fog feels like pushing a boundary, fast travel to revealed tiles within 3 keeps backtracking painless, and blockages always offer 2+ solutions. The curiosity whisper closes the last gap — now there's a reason to slow down and look: hint → examine → story → codex. Examination costs are honest (0 kcal, 2 ticks, time-only, verified against the real clock).

## Open tensions (Steve calls, not worker fixes)

- `tileFeature` density (~13/tile) might be generous once the codex fills — but features are one-shot reveals, so it self-limits.
