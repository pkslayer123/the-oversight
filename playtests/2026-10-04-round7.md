## Harness notes (bot limitations, not game bugs)

1. **`doAction('forage')` returns null without advancing time when no plant cell is within reach** — correct game behavior (you can't forage thin air; the player taps a specific 🌱 cell), but an early version of this harness spun without advancing days. Fixed by falling back to `doAction('wait')` on null.
2. **Bots don't micro-move in the 9×9 detail grid.** Being on a tile with stock > 0 doesn't guarantee an adjacent plant cell at the bot's (mx,my). Several bots recorded 0 successful forages over 12 days and lived off village meals or died — a harness pathfinding gap (round 4's bots used BFS in the detail grid). Human players tap plant cells directly.
3. **Phase-2 bots claim only the first ability offer** (first choice, no slot management). Post-arrival slot/XP depth is better covered by round 4's bots.

## Trust behavior in the wild

Talker bots talked 20–40 times per run across 12 days; NPC trust rose toward 40 and never exceeded it via conversation, and never dropped. The 40-cap holds under sustained talking, and earned trust (via food gifts) is no longer destroyed by chatting.
