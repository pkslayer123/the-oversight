# Village Meal Rebalance Test — 2026-10-03

**Question:** Does the village meal system fix starvation? Do players survive 10 days now?

**Answer: Yes.** All three characters survived 10 days. In the previous round, all three died of starvation by day 5–7 regardless of strategy.

Note: rations overshoot slightly because the code takes whole units (`ceil(need/kcalEach)`). Half ration lands at 1050–1200 depending on which item is pulled. Fine — it's honest whole-food math, not a bug.

## Playthroughs: 10 days each — all survived

| Character | Strategy | Days | kcal | Health | Hydration | Pantry |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jesse (hunter) | Forage/hunt expeditions | 10 ✅ | 2440–3000 | 38–83 | 45 | 34500 → 25600 |
| Mara (doctor) | Stay at Haven, rely on meals | 10 ✅ | 3000 (cap) | 100 | 45 | 34500 → 21600 |
| Theo (selfish) | Hoard pantry, minimal effort | 10 ✅ | 3000 (cap) | 100 | 45 | 34500 → 22000 |

**Mara is the headline.** She did nothing but wait at Haven for 10 days — zero foraging — and stayed at full health and full kcal on village meals alone. The design thesis works: you're one of the 12, the village feeds you, your job is to contribute.

**Jesse's runs varied with RNG** (map/encounters): one run foraged 5× with health 83, another foraged 0× with health 38 (took damage from something — monster or risky water — but survived). Both lived. The variance is fine; the floor holds.

**Pantry sustainability:** 10 days cost the village ~9–13k kcal total (~1,000–1,300/day for all 12). Villagers are mostly self-sufficient (they forage, eat, share surplus by trust); the player's meal is the main pantry draw. At this burn rate the starting 34.5k lasts ~4 weeks. Survival pressure comes from the *player's* contribution and the *village's* long-term balance, not day-3 starvation. That's the intended shift.

## Bugs found and fixed during testing (3)

1. **`drinkWater()` never restored hydration.** The bottle-system rewrite consumed a bottle but didn't touch `hydration`. Everyone would still have died of dehydration by ~day 7 regardless of meals. Fixed: +50 hydration per drink (cap 100). Also removed a dead duplicate `drinkWater` definition (the old `kcal += 0` stub was shadowed but confusing).
2. **`doAction('treat')` corrupted the water array.** `scholar.water = (scholar.water || 0) + 2` on an array of bottle objects produces `"[object Object],[object Object]2"` — a string. Not reachable from the current UI, but a landmine for any caller. Fixed: pushes 2 clean bottle objects.
3. **`villageMeal()` destroyed food at the kcal cap.** Scholar at 2900 + meal pulls 2400 from pantry → scholar capped at 3000, **2,300 kcal vanishes**. Verified live. Fixed: meal only takes `min(share, 3000 - kcal)`; says "You're full" when there's no room.

## Design gaps (not bugs — need Steve's call)

1. **Half-ration path is unreachable in natural play.** `newGame` sets `trust[villagerId] = 100` ("you trust yourself"), and `villageMeal` reads that same entry. The player always gets full+bonus (2200). The trust<30 half-ration mechanic only fires if something lowers the player's own trust entry — nothing currently does. If the intent is "the village watches newcomers," the meal should read a *village-trusts-player* value, not the self-trust entry. As implemented, every player is a trusted insider from day 1.
2. **Meals don't include water.** Realistic (you still have to drink), but both Haven-bound bots would have died of dehydration without manual water management. The Haven well fiction works (`fillWater` at Haven → clean), but there's no UI prompt tying "eat your meal" to "drink your water." Watch for this in phone playtesting.
3. **Theo the selfish still thrives.** He hoarded pantry food *and* got full meals *and* ended at 100 health. There's currently no social cost to selfishness in the sim (no trust decay for hoarding, no one confronts him). The trust-gated ration is the lever for this — but see gap #1.

## Method

Node harness (`/tmp/meal-test.js`): stubbed `fetch` + `localStorage`, eval'd engine + game.js, `Game.init()`. Note: the task brief said `Game.loadData()` — the actual API is `Game.init()`. Day loop: 4 `doAction` calls/day (with stall-guard: `doAction` returns null early without consuming a day part when e.g. nothing is forageable, so the bot falls back to `wait`). Bots drank at hydration<60 and restocked bottles. Two full runs per character (RNG variance noted).

## Verdict

The rebalance works. Starvation is fixed — not by tuning numbers, but by changing the model: the village feeds you, you contribute. All three strategies survive 10 days. The remaining work is the trust wiring (gap #1) and the social consequences of selfishness (gap #3), which are design decisions, not bugs.
