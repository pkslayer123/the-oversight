# Playtest: Alive Villagers + Creature Personality (2026-10-04)

## What was built

**Villager needs system** — hunger/fear/social/energy per NPC, ticking every day part.
Moods derived from dominant need + village state (grieving, scared, hungry, grateful,
cold, cheerful, lonely, weary, steady). Behavior from wants, not dice.

**Villager initiative** — NPCs approach the player on their own (1/day-part max):
- hungry → asks for food (sets a request; answered = gratitude + trust, ignored = remembered coldness, trust -2)
- scared → seeks fire/company
- lonely → starts conversation (gossip, complaints, grief-aware lines)
- grateful → repays kindness (teaches a plant, restores energy, adds to pantry)

**Memory** — `village.memory[rid]`: gifts, ignored asks, etc. Recent kindness → grateful
mood; recent indifference → cold. Trust drifts accordingly.

**Village events** — monster attack → fear spike; death → 3 days grief; big donation →
cheer; combat victory at haven → fear relief. Ambient social beats in villageLives():
arguments, laughter, humming, grief-quiet, unprompted pantry contributions.

**Monster stance machine** — curious / territorial / hungry / cautious / fearful / ambush,
driven by data (behavior, aggression, fear) + stimuli (fire, daylight, group size,
proximity). Nightlight only hunts near water at night. Speedbump never moves until
you're close. Territorial warns once, then charges. Curious may lose interest or
escalate. Fearful flees and despawns.

**Creature ambiguity** — encounters announce descriptors ("Something moves out there —
a deer, standing too still"), not names. Codex observed stage on survive/flee/rout or
combat start. Popup shows vibe + name only after known. Wandering monster renders its
own emoji (was hardcoded boar).

**Animal aliveness** — graze (dist≥4) / freeze-and-assess (dist 2-3, "ears up, deciding
about you") / bolt (dist<2). They want to survive, not fight you.

## Verification

  identification, all 10 stance mappings, stance transitions, initiative trigger,
  request set, rabbit graze/alert/bolt
- Content gate: OK (all counts unchanged)
- scripts/simulate.js: clean (random 0% / greedy 10% win — pre-existing balance)
- scripts/simulate-combat.js: clean, no crashes across all 10 monsters

## Known limitations
- Initiative lines are template pools (3-5 per need); will repeat over long runs
- Grateful "teach" uses identifyPlant directly — generous but maybe too generous
- Monster cue cooldown is global per monster (5 turns); fine for now
- No villager-vs-villager needs (they don't feed each other yet)
