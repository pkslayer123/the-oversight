# Brawler run — 2026-10-04 (evening)

Archetype 4: human combat, intimidation, theft, village justice escalation.

## Finding: theft and intimidation didn't exist as player verbs

The brawler had Attack (two-tap, traumatic, justice-linked) but no way to steal
or threaten. Steve's rule is "theft allowed, socially punished" — and there was
no theft at all. NPCs don't carry personal food inventories, so there was
nothing to steal from. Fixed:

- `packKcal(vid)`: each villager carries a daily ration (~200–1400 kcal),
  reseeds at dawn. Hidden — no numbers in UI; victims feel hunger, not ledgers.
- `stealFrom(vid)`: rifle their pack. 1 tick. Detection by temperament
  (cautious +20%, night −15%, close quarters +10%). Caught = no food, trust
  cratered, `observe('theft')` + justice crime. Unseen = you get the rations,
  but the victim notices a part later (hunger audits) → suspicion, crime,
  gossip seed. That's the detective hook.
- `intimidate(vid)`: two-tap "your food, now." 2 ticks. Temperament-driven:
  cautious yields terrified (fear +40, trust −40); steady refuses flat
  ("Ask. Like a person."); bold pushes back — desperate ones swing
  (npcBetrays), others just hate you and tell everyone.
- justice heat: theft +15, intimidation +15 each; amends credit wears them
  down (unlike murder). The comment in justiceHeat already promised theft
  heat — it just never implemented it. Now it does.
- Person card: `🤏 Lift rations` + `👊 Intimidate` (two-tap) buttons.

Tests: scripts/test-theft.js (33/33). Regressions: justice 43/43,

## Feel verdict

Tense and fun. The steal is a real gamble — 35% base detection means you're
sweating every time, and night raids feel meaningfully safer. The delayed
notice is the delight: you walk away clean, then next part someone's
slowly going through their pack and their eyes keep finding you. Intimidating
a cautious villager feels awful in the right way (their hands shake; the game
doesn't congratulate you). Bold villagers swinging back keeps it from being
a free-food button — it's a fight you didn't want.

Friction noted while playing: none new. The loop is complete enough that a
brawler playthrough now has an arc: steal → get suspected → get confronted
→ intimidate → village turns cold → justice vote.
