## Feel verdict (survivalist, wild camp — 6 days, no haven return)

**The loop works now, and it's honestly hard.** Two fixes this run:

1. **The pantry teleported.** The dawn village meal (+1000–2000 kcal, +1L clean
   water) reached the player anywhere on the map, and the player's roster draw
   in villageEats drained the pantry for an absent mouth. Wild camping was
   free — the whole fire/boil/forage chore loop was redundant. Fixed: the
   pantry is physical (consistent with the haven stores gate). No meal and no
   roster draw while away; one honest line: "You camp wild tonight — no pantry
   meal. Eat from your pack." Membership still has no check-ins — you stay a
   member, you just don't get fed. (Tests: scripts/test-pantry-physical.js,
   13 pass.)

2. **The cautious test was camp-only.** Steve's design says the edibility test
   is "always available, always honest" — but the UI only offered it in the
   haven stash. In the field, unknown lumps were dead weight: the camper
   starved at 0 kcal surrounded by food they couldn't identify, with the game
   saying "identify it first" and no way to do it. Fixed: the carried pack now
   offers Test cautiously / Rush it / Watch the fauna on lumps anywhere.
   Sorting stays camp-only (flat surface, good light, knowledgeable people —
   honest). The eat() hint now says "test cautiously from your pack, or sort
   it at camp". (Tests: scripts/test-field-identify.js, 11 pass.)

**Post-fix wild camp:** 6 days, ended kcal=46, hp=100, 14L clean water, 2
fires from 10 attempts (8 honest failures, each teaching), 3 boils, fireside
sleeps when earned. The camper survives on knowledge — forage unknowns, spend
an afternoon testing, eat what the test clears. Tight but survivable: exactly
the "slow and risky" start. Fire reliability before the knack (3 successes)
feels punishing (4 failed attempts in a row on D2, cold-ground sleep) but each
failure names what went wrong, and the knack is one success away — acceptable
by design, watch in future loops.

**Player delights:** fire failure lines ("Sparks, then nothing. The tinder's
too coarse — shred it finer next time." / "The coal glows... and dies.
Closer. You're closer."), "Boiled 6L. Bacteria dead.", the game's coaching
("work the green, haul it back, get it named at camp. That's the whole
game.").
