# Hunter playtest — 2026-10-04 (loop run, archetype 5)

## Played
Full hunter loop in sim: trap crafting knowledge gating → 2-trap trapline over 12
dawns → 180 active stalks (rabbit/turkey/deer) → night hunting with L2 knowledge →
wild monster wanderer + RNG encounter rates.

## BUG FOUND + FIXED: away-tile traps never checked
`checkTraps()` only swept `playerTile()` — a trap set anywhere you weren't
standing at dawn NEVER caught anything. Measured: home-tile snare 5 catches/12
dawns; away-tile snare 0/12. A trapline away from camp was dead weight.

Fix (src/js/game.js): `checkTraps()` now sweeps all 49 tiles at dawn. Catch
messages name the location ("Your Snare 1 tile north caught a Cottontail
Rabbit!") so the food never arrives silently. Per-tile `setDay` gating and
use-degradation preserved; spent traps are removed from their own tile.
Test: scripts/test-trapline.js (6/6 pass).

Balance note: one snare ≈ 30–40% catch/day → ~2,875 kcal per 12 days passive
(rabbit 800 / squirrel 350, carcasses still need knife cleaning + spoil fast).
A 3-trap line ≈ 700 kcal/day passive — strong but bounded by 10 uses/trap,
crafting time (32 ticks each), materials, and the cleaning chain. Tuning lever
is the 0.4/day rate if traplines trivialize foraging. This matches Steve's
"Hunting capacity = trap supplies + knowledge" and "traps must be stronger
than ~2 uses."

## Measurements
- Craft gating: blind craft blocked, L2 → craft → L3 via practice. Good.
- Active hunting (adjacent, spear range 2, no tracker): rabbit 31/60 got
  (26 bolted), turkey 21/60 (29 bolted), deer 15/60 (37 bolted). Bolt rates
  match preyWariness design (0.45/0.6/0.75 − 0.10 point blank). Feels right:
  stalking is tense, misses cost, tracker ability matters.
- Night hunting: L2 night_hunting granted → fox(medium) 28/60 at night vs
  turkey 21/60 by day. Bonus is real and measurable.
- Wild encounters: thicket 29/200 (design 15%), meadow 8/200 (design 5%),
  ruin 30/200 (design 12%). No true-name leak in telegraphs.
- Wanderer (Bulldozer boar): patrols from day 3, pendingEncounter on contact,
  villages correctly safe.

## Feel verdict
Hunter loop is now coherent: craft (knowledge-gated) → set a line → sleep →
dawn report with locations → clean/preserve carcasses. Active hunting stays
the high-risk/high-reward play (deer = 20,000 kcal carcass, 25% net per
stalk). Night is meaningfully the hunter's time. Fun, not chores — the
trapline finally does what the fiction promises.
