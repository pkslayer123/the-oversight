# THE FIVE ITEMS

Everyone arrives with five personally important/reliable items plus favorite clothes. No loot drops exist in this game — these five are your entire material inheritance, and through relic bonding they become your endgame arsenal. Gear is grown, not found.

## Acquisition (onboarding, v1)

Second onboarding beat after "where is home": **"The sky is changing. You can carry five things. What do you grab?"** Guided pick from the catalog (`items.json`), organized by class. Freeform within classes — the mix of practical and sentimental is the point, not a rigid slot formula. Suggested shape: ~2 tools, ~1 practical clothing, ~1 sentimental, ~1 wildcard (the most personal — starts with no mechanical effect, has the deepest bond track and the strangest enhancements).

## Classes and enhancement pools

Every item has a class. Class determines its enhancement pool — standard mechanical bones, narrative skin.

**Tool** (knife, lighter, multitool, rope, fishing line...)
- *Efficiency:* faster/better at its job (25% yield or speed)
- *Durability:* never dulls, never breaks, never runs out (within reason — the lighter's fuel is story-limited, not counter-limited)
- *Impossible edge:* physics-defying — cuts frozen meat like butter, lights in driving rain, rope that never tangles

**Clothing** (boots, jacket, gloves...)
- *Protection:* weather armor — ignore rain/cold penalties
- *Comfort:* energy recovery, morale on the trail
- *Camouflage:* harder to detect, by animals and otherwise

**Sentimental** (drawing, ring, photo, letter...)
- *Resolve:* willpower — once per expedition, ignore a despair/morale break
- *Quiet luck:* small favorable nudges the System cannot explain ("statistical anomaly detected. We choose not to investigate.")
- *Anchor:* prevents spiral — when health would hit zero from despair, hold at 1 (once per season)

## Bond (passive, never a grind)

Bond accrues through normal play. No meters to manage, no grinding — the game notices, you don't.

- **Tools/clothing:** +1 bond per day of meaningful use (capped 1/day/item — carrying it doesn't count, *using* it does).
- **Sentimental:** +1 per day simply kept in your pack, +3 at story moments (survived the thing you were afraid of while carrying it).
- **Thresholds:** 10 / 25 / 50. At each, the System offers an optimization — **pick 1 of 3** enhancements from the class pool.
- The System's commentary is mandatory and earnest: *"We have detected elevated attachment to Unit K-NIFE. This is inefficient. This is also... [PROCESSING] ...valuable? Optimization available."*

## Loss (story, not durability)

No durability bars (simplicity doctrine). Items are lost at story moments — dropped in a river crossing, left at a camp you fled, broken shielding someone. Losing a bonded relic is a real event: morale hit, village mourns with you, the System attempts condolences (*"Unit K-NIFE has ceased function. It was a good knife."*). Stakes without micromanagement.

## Broken-build rule

Relic + ability combos may break a single dimension (unkillable in combat, infinite food preservation) but never the stomach. Every combo is tested against: **"does this let you skip dinner?"**

## Worked example: Mara Okafor's five

1. **Multitool** (tool) — opens one-handed, +1 forage yield edge. Bond 10: *Efficient Joints* (25% faster camp tasks) / *Never Sticks* (works in mud/ice) / *Impossible Edge* (cuts bone clean).
2. **Lighter** (tool) — fire on demand. Bond 25: *Rainproof Flame* (lights in any weather) / *Eternal Wick* (never needs fuel — the System is *very* confused by this one) / *Signal Bright* (visible for miles).
3. **Field suture kit** (tool) — stabilize one severe injury per expedition. Bond 10: *Steady Kit* (+1 use) / *Clean Close* (no infection risk) / *Borrowed Time* (stabilize at 0 health once).
4. **Daughter's drawing** (sentimental) — +resolve when carried. Bond 21 days: *Anchor* (hold at 1 health vs despair, once/season) / *Quiet Luck* (small nudges) / *Her Voice* (morale restore, once/expedition — *"You can hear her. Keep going."*).
5. **Good boots** (clothing) — +1 travel AP/day. Bond 25: *Second Skin* (no blisters, ever) / *Sure Footing* (no travel injuries) / *Pathfinder* (the boots find footing that isn't there — 1 free travel leg/day).
