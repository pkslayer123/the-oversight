# End-Game Builds — Paths to Functional Godhood

Steve's directives (2026-10-07):
- *"Paint a path towards functional godhood. If they learn and experiment enough, people can get there and it's scary good. Most game wins look like that, but not all."*
- *"More than just one path though. Don't lock a player down one path to godhood."*
- *"Let each paint a path towards functionally being humanity's representative at the galactic table. Each must inspire that level of magnificence."*

## Design principles

- **Multiple paths per type.** Every player type has 2-3 distinct godhood paths. Different fantasies, different kits, different gear. No one is locked into a single destiny.
- **Galactic-representative magnificence.** Every path should inspire: *this person could sit at the galactic table and represent humanity.* Not just "really good at surviving" — MAGNIFICENT. The kind of being aliens respect, fear, or want to recruit.
- **Transcendence, not replacement.** Players already have great survival abilities by late game. Godhood doesn't replace those — it transcends them. The forager who fed a village becomes the one who makes scarcity a rumor.
- **Discoverable, not railroaded.** No quest chains push players down a path. The pieces exist in the world; synergies are logical and guessable; players connect the dots.
- **Earned, not given.** Every build needs late-game gear (tier 3-4), a full 6-ability kit, and at least one synergy. You don't stumble into godhood on day 12.
- **Scary good, not invincible.** Each build dominates its domain and has a real weakness.
- **Many roads.** These are examples, not a menu. Players will find builds we didn't imagine — that's the point.

## 1. Socialite

### 1a. The Shepherd of the Dead

**The fantasy:** Death is not the end of your party — it's a promotion. Fallen allies fight on as echoes. Enemies convert mid-battle, laying down arms to join the winning side: yours. Your party *creates its own members* — every battle makes you stronger. When the aliens came to harvest humanity, they found you leading an army that death itself couldn't thin. At the galactic table, you don't negotiate from strength. You negotiate from *inevitability*.

**Ability kit (6):** diplomat, mediator, pact, grave_robber, hive_mind, generous
**Key synergies:** Peacemaker's Voice (diplomat+mediator) — enemies stand down; Delver (scrounger→grave_robber) — the dead have uses
**Gear path:**
- Early: any loud clothing, a decent melee weapon — you're just charismatic
- Mid: whisper_catchers (acc, T2) — hear everything, know everyone's secrets
- Late: death_mask (head, T3) — speak with the recently dead; war_horn (acc, T3) — your party fears nothing
- Godhood: commanders_coat (torso, T4) + banner_of_the_fallen (acc, T4) — the fallen fight on, your party has no ceiling
**How it plays:** You walk into a monster den with six friends and walk out with eight. Converts, echoes, and absolute loyalty. Weakness: alone, you're just a talker.

### 1b. The Galactic Diplomat

**The fantasy:** The aliens came to conquer and stayed to negotiate — because you were there. You've ended wars with words, turned invasions into trade agreements, talked a sadistic trophy hunter into putting down the spear. At the galactic table, humanity doesn't need a warrior. It needs *you* — the one every species would rather have talking than fighting. Your voice is humanity's shield.

**Ability kit (6):** diplomat, mediator, translator, open_book, fear_aura, generous
**Key synergies:** Peacemaker's Voice (diplomat+mediator); Polyglot's Ledger (translator+open_book) — nothing is lost in translation
**Gear path:**
- Early: learn every language you can, including the ones no one speaks anymore
- Mid: whisper_catchers (acc, T2) — hear what isn't said
- Late: peace_accords (acc, T3) — your treaties bind
- Godhood: universal_translator (acc, T4) — speak to anything, and be understood
**How it plays:** Fights end before they start. The scariest monster in the game backs down because you *asked*. Weakness: when words fail — and sometimes they do — you need someone else's fists.

### 1c. The Consensus

**The fantasy:** You are not one voice. You are *the* voice — the living will of your people, braided into a single mind. Your party doesn't follow orders; they share thoughts. Decisions happen at the speed of thinking. The aliens have hive species. They've never seen a human do this voluntarily, joyfully, *lovingly*. You are proof that humanity's greatest strength was never the individual. It was each other.

**Ability kit (6):** hive_mind, ant_trail, mediator, generous, symbiote, pact
**Key synergies:** One Mind (hive_mind+ant_trail) — the party is one organism
**Gear path:**
- Early: be the person everyone trusts — that's the whole foundation
- Mid: neural threads, shared meals, shared danger
- Late: neural_weave (acc, T3) — share senses across distance
- Godhood: consensus_crown (head, T4) — your party acts as one mind in combat
**How it plays:** Perfect coordination. Flanks that happen without signals, rescues before the cry for help. Weakness: the mind-link is two-way — their pain is yours, all of it, at once.

## 2. Hunter

### 2a. Apex Predator

**The fantasy:** Nothing escapes you. You track anything across any terrain, and when you loose, it dies. The Highbeam Deer — the benchmark, the nightmare — fears *you*. The aliens study apex predators across a thousand worlds. They've added you to the list, and you're the only entry that hunts *them* back.

**Ability kit (6):** tracker, echo_location, patient_aim, dead_aim, game_sense, soft_step
**Key synergies:** Apex Sense (tracker+echo_location) — you always know where it is
**Gear path:**
- Early: a bow, patience
- Mid: trackers_blind (acc, T2) — they never see you coming
- Late: ghost_camo (torso, T3), heartseeker_arrows (ammo, T3), predators_trophy (acc, T3)
- Godhood: apex_bow (ranged, T4) — at close range, you do not miss
**How it plays:** You choose the fight, the range, the moment. Everything else is logistics. Weakness: if it reaches you, you're just a person with a bow.

### 2b. The Ghost

**The fantasy:** They never saw you. That's the point. You kill from nowhere — no sound, no trace, no warning. The aliens have sensors that see through walls, satellites that track heat, drones that never blink. None of it matters. You are the argument that humanity doesn't need to be seen to be deadly. At the galactic table, the empty chair is yours — and everyone checks over their shoulder.

**Ability kit (6):** soft_step, night_eyes, patient_aim, dead_aim, echo_location, cold_blooded
**Key synergies:** Nowhere Man (soft_step+cold_blooded) — no heat, no sound, no sign
**Gear path:**
- Early: move quiet, strike once
- Mid: trackers_blind (acc, T2)
- Late: whisper_rounds (acc, T3) — the shot makes no sound; ghost_camo (torso, T3)
- Godhood: void_cloak (torso, T4) — sensors can't see you. Nothing can.
**How it plays:** The fight is over before anyone knows it started. You don't win battles — you delete them. Weakness: a stand-up fight. If they ever *find* you, you're in trouble.

### 2c. The Beastmaster

**The fantasy:** The wild doesn't fear you. It *follows* you. Monsters, animals, the whole ecosystem — they fight for you, warn you, die for you. You're not humanity's hunter. You're Earth's voice, and Earth has opinions about the aliens. When Vex Marlowe came to hunt trophies, she found the trophies hunting *her* — because you asked them to.

**Ability kit (6):** game_sense, tracker, bird_whisperer, squirrel_friend, fear_aura, pact
**Key synergies:** Wild Court (bird_whisperer+squirrel_friend+pact) — the wild is your constituency
**Gear path:**
- Early: feed everything, learn every call
- Mid: run with packs, earn their trust
- Late: wildmark_wraps (hands, T3) — animals read you as kin
- Godhood: beastcall_horn (acc, T4) — call the wild to war
**How it plays:** You're never alone. The forest is your army. Weakness: the wild has its own needs — neglect them and the alliance frays.

## 3. Forager

### 3a. The Living Garden

**The fantasy:** The land provides. You want for nothing — food, medicine, materials flow from your hands. The village eats because you walked the fields. The aliens terraform with machines over centuries, at staggering cost. You do it with your hands, in a season, for free. When humanity sits at the galactic table, you are the proof that we don't need their technology. We have something better: we *belong* here.

**Ability kit (6):** green_thumb, photosynthesis, compost_king, dowsing, forage_identification, camp_cook
**Key synergies:** Sun Eater's Garden (green_thumb+photosynthesis) — you are fed by light; Closed Loop (camp_cook+compost_king) — nothing is wasted
**Gear path:**
- Early: knowledge. That's the whole build at first.
- Mid: rain_caller_charm (acc, T2)
- Late: living_soil_robes (torso, T3), harvest_scythe (melee, T3)
- Godhood: seed_vault (acc, T4) — plant anything, anywhere; feast_king_cauldron (acc, T3) — feed armies
**How it plays:** Scarcity is a rumor. You feed the village, stock the pantry, and trade surplus for everything else. Weakness: you're not a fighter — your garden needs guards.

### 3b. The Terraformer

**The fantasy:** You don't find fertile land. You *make* it. Dead zones bloom in your wake — ash becomes soil, desert becomes garden. The aliens have a word for what you do. It translates roughly to "god-work," and they don't use it lightly. Whole regions of Earth are alive because you walked through them. At the galactic table, you don't represent humanity's strength. You represent its *generosity* — the species that heals worlds.

**Ability kit (6):** green_thumb, compost_king, dowsing, rain_dancer, photosynthesis, taught_hands
**Key synergies:** Worldshaper (green_thumb+rain_dancer+dowsing) — land obeys
**Gear path:**
- Early: heal one blighted patch. Then another.
- Mid: rain_caller_charm (acc, T2)
- Late: rain_engine (acc, T3) — convince the sky; living_soil_robes (torso, T3)
- Godhood: worldseed_satchel (acc, T4) — Earth's backup, in your pocket
**How it plays:** The map changes because of you. Permanently. Weakness: terraforming takes time — you can't do it mid-fight.

### 3c. The Preserver

**The fantasy:** When Earth is gone — if Earth is gone — you are its memory. You carry the seeds and knowledge of every plant, every recipe, every growing thing. Nothing goes extinct on your watch. The aliens collect species in vaults and databases. You carry ten thousand species in a box you can hold, and you know every one by name. You are the living library of a world.

**Ability kit (6):** forage_identification, preservation_instinct, taught_hands, field_dressing, deep_reserves, extra_stomach
**Key synergies:** Living Archive (forage_identification+preservation_instinct+taught_hands) — nothing is lost
**Gear path:**
- Early: learn every plant. Press every leaf.
- Mid: seedkeepers_grimoire (acc, T3) — identify anything on sight
- Late: deep cellars, labeled jars, redundant caches
- Godhood: memory_seed_vault (acc, T4) — ten thousand species, in your hands
**How it plays:** You are irreplaceable. Kill you and humanity loses a world. Everyone knows it — including the aliens. Weakness: the vault makes you a target. Everyone wants what you carry.

## 4. Survivalist

### 4a. The Unkillable

**The fantasy:** The world tries to kill you and fails. Beams, beasts, starvation, cold — you've survived all of it and you'll survive what's next. The aliens have tested weapons on you. The weapons broke. At the galactic table, you don't need to threaten anyone. You just need to *be there* — living proof that humanity cannot be killed, only inconvenienced.

**Ability kit (6):** phoenix_clause, second_wind, survivor, chitin_skin, molt, iron_stomach
**Key synergies:** Refuses Death (phoenix_clause+second_wind) — death is a suggestion; Perfect Chrysalis (chitin_skin+molt) — every wound makes you harder
**Gear path:**
- Early: layered clothing, any armor
- Mid: deep_pocket_coat (torso, T2) — carry what you need to endure
- Late: scar_tissue_wraps (hands, T3), deep_earth_boots (shoes, T3)
- Godhood: worldroot_plate (torso full-body, T4) OR full alien armor set — walk through beams
**How it plays:** You outlast. Sieges, winters, alien hunters — you're still standing. Weakness: you're a wall, not a weapon. Things you can't outlast (politics, betrayal) still hurt.

### 4b. The Adaptation

**The fantasy:** Everything that tried to kill you made you stronger. *Literally.* Your body learns — poison, beams, cold, hunger, fear. You've survived them all, and now they can't touch you. The aliens study you like a miracle because you *are* one: a human who evolved in real time. They have laboratories that try to do what your body does naturally. They fail.

**Ability kit (6):** chitin_skin, molt, iron_stomach, extra_stomach, second_skin, symbiote
**Key synergies:** Perfect Chrysalis (chitin_skin+molt); Living Immunity (iron_stomach+extra_stomach+symbiote)
**Gear path:**
- Early: survive things. That's the training.
- Mid: toxin_library (acc, T3) — everything that tried to poison you, catalogued
- Late: second_skin_suit — shed what hurts you
- Godhood: adaptive_dermis (torso, T4) — your skin learns from damage
**How it plays:** The longer the fight goes, the harder you are to hurt. Weakness: the *first* hit of a new damage type still lands. Novelty is your enemy.

### 4c. The Shelter

**The fantasy:** You don't just survive. Everyone near you survives. Cold, hunger, fear, monsters — they can't touch those under your care. The aliens bombed a village once. You were in it. Everyone lived. They don't understand how. You do: you were *there*, and that was enough. At the galactic table, you are humanity's proof that survival isn't selfish. It's *shared*.

**Ability kit (6):** survivor, camp_cook, second_wind, generous, triage, steady_hands
**Key synergies:** Hearth (camp_cook+generous+survivor) — your presence is survival
**Gear path:**
- Early: keep people fed, keep people warm
- Mid: big fires, bigger pots
- Late: shelter_cloak (torso, T3) — carry someone through the storm
- Godhood: hearthstone_amulet (acc, T4) — your presence is a haven
**How it plays:** Your party is unkillable by attrition. Sieges break against you. Weakness: you can't be everywhere. The people you can't reach are on their own.

## 5. Brawler

### 5a. One-Person Army

**The fantasy:** Walk into impossible fights and walk out. The more they hurt you, the more dangerous you become. Rage as a renewable resource. The aliens deploy squads, then platoons, then stop deploying anything — because you keep walking out of the kill zone, and it's embarrassing them. At the galactic table, you don't represent humanity's wisdom. You represent its *refusal*.

**Ability kit (6):** rage, second_wind, blood_magic, leech, adrenaline_control, cornered_rat
**Key synergies:** Undying Fury (rage+second_wind); Crimson Circuit (blood_magic+leech) — their blood is your health; Cornered Fury (adrenaline_control+cornered_rat)
**Gear path:**
- Early: the biggest blunt object you can lift
- Mid: rage is free — learn to get hurt productively
- Late: rage_bindings (hands, T3), skull_helm (head, T3)
- Godhood: worldbreaker_maul (melee, T4) + bloodplate (torso, T4) — every hit you take is a hit you'll give back
**How it plays:** You are the plan. No tactics, no retreat — just forward. Weakness: anything that doesn't bleed (beams, politics, starvation) ignores your whole build.

### 5b. The Living Weapon

**The fantasy:** You don't need gear. You *are* the gear. No sword, no armor — just you. The aliens wear powered exoskeletons, energy shields, combat drugs. You broke an exoskeleton with your hands. They're still trying to figure out how. At the galactic table, you sit down, and everyone quietly recalculates humanity's threat rating.

**Ability kit (6):** rage, adrenaline_control, chitin_skin, iron_stomach, second_wind, cornered_rat
**Key synergies:** Body of War (chitin_skin+adrenaline_control) — flesh as armor, will as weapon
**Gear path:**
- Early: train. Your body is the project.
- Mid: titan_belt (acc, T3) — the last gear you'll ever need
- Late: shed the armor. You don't need it.
- Godhood: ironfist_wraps (hands, T4) — your fists are tier-4 weapons
**How it plays:** Disarm you and you've lost nothing. Capture you and you've lost nothing. You are the weapon. Weakness: you can't lend your body to someone else. Your power doesn't scale to armies.

### 5c. The Warlord

**The fantasy:** Armies follow you — not because you ordered them to, but because you've never asked them to do what you wouldn't do yourself. You lead from the front. The first through the breach, the last to retreat. The aliens respect strength above all things, and you are the strongest thing they've seen that bleeds. When you speak at the galactic table, the room listens — because everyone there has heard what happens to people who don't.

**Ability kit (6):** rage, fear_aura, adrenaline_control, second_wind, pact, generous
**Key synergies:** Crimson Command (fear_aura+pact) — loyalty through shared violence
**Gear path:**
- Early: fight beside people, not above them
- Mid: earn the scars visibly
- Late: warlords_standard (acc, T4) — your banner is a promise
- Godhood: conquerors_plate (torso, T4) — dented in forty places, each one someone who tried
**How it plays:** Your militia fights like a legion. Morale is a weapon and you're holding it. Weakness: the army needs you *there*. Without you, they're just people.

## 6. Detective

### 6a. The All-Seeing

**The fantasy:** You know everything. Every lie, every hidden thing, every move before it's made. Knowledge isn't power — it's *control*. The aliens have surveillance networks spanning star systems. You have eyes, ears, and a mind that doesn't miss. They've tried to deceive you. It hasn't worked yet. At the galactic table, you are the one who makes sure everyone is telling the truth — because you always know when they're not.

**Ability kit (6):** third_eye, pattern_recognition, open_book, translator, eyes_in_back, night_eyes
**Key synergies:** Sees the Weave (third_eye+pattern_recognition); Polyglot's Ledger (translator+open_book)
**Gear path:**
- Early: talk to everyone, read everything
- Mid: whisper_catchers (acc, T2)
- Late: investigators_coat (torso, T3)
- Godhood: truth_lenses (head, T4) + the_ledger (acc, T4) — nothing hidden, nothing forgotten
**How it plays:** You dismantle plots before they form, turn enemies' plans against them, and always know the third option. Weakness: knowing the punch is coming doesn't mean you can take it.

### 6b. The Weaver

**The fantasy:** You don't predict the future. You *choose* it. You see causality itself — not just what's hidden, but what's *coming*, and what leads to what. The aliens have predictive engines the size of cities. They can't model you, because you keep changing the variables. Every plan you touch bends toward your design. At the galactic table, you don't argue. You just... arrange things. And they happen.

**Ability kit (6):** third_eye, pattern_recognition, time_skip, echo_location, open_book, night_eyes
**Key synergies:** Sees the Weave (third_eye+pattern_recognition); Thread-Puller (time_skip+pattern_recognition)
**Gear path:**
- Early: notice patterns. Write them down.
- Mid: test predictions. Get them right.
- Late: fate_threads (acc, T3) — feel which threads are taut
- Godhood: causality_lens (head, T4) — see what leads to what
**How it plays:** You're always three moves ahead. Ambushes are invitations. Traps are your traps now. Weakness: seeing the future doesn't let you be in two places. Some threads you can't pull in time.

### 6c. The Confessor

**The fantasy:** No one lies to you. *Ever.* You strip away falsehood — every lie confessed, every mask dropped, every hidden motive dragged into the light. Aliens fear your questions more than weapons. Tyrants, con artists, infiltrators — they all break in front of you. At the galactic table, you are the guarantee: when humanity gives its word, it's true. Because you're there to make sure.

**Ability kit (6):** open_book, translator, pattern_recognition, fear_aura, mediator, third_eye
**Key synergies:** No Masks (open_book+fear_aura) — falsehood cannot stand before you
**Gear path:**
- Early: ask questions. Listen to the answers. Listen to what's *not* said.
- Mid: build a reputation — people confess to you voluntarily
- Late: truthbrand (melee, T3) — it cuts pretense, not flesh
- Godhood: confessional_seal (acc, T4) — no one can lie to you. No one.
**How it plays:** Every negotiation, every interrogation, every trial — you win before it starts. Weakness: truth without power is just information. You need someone to *act* on what you learn.

## 7. Miser

### 7a. The Market God

**The fantasy:** Infinite resources. The market bends to you. You don't find what you need — it finds you, at your price. The aliens came to exploit Earth's resources. They ended up trading *with* you, on your terms, and they're still not sure how that happened. At the galactic table, you don't represent humanity's military or its wisdom. You represent its *economy* — and the economy always wins.

**Ability kit (6):** hoarder, scrounger, grave_robber, thief, squirrel_friend, bird_whisperer
**Key synergies:** Delver (scrounger+grave_robber); Critter Network (bird_whisperer+squirrel_friend) — eyes everywhere
**Gear path:**
- Early: pick up everything. Everything.
- Mid: misers_eye (acc, T2) — know what it's worth instantly; deep_pocket_coat (torso, T2)
- Late: gilded_pack (acc, T3), merchants_seals (acc, T3)
- Godhood: vault_key (acc, T4) — your caches are everywhere; you are never without
**How it plays:** You have the right thing at the right time, always. Trade your way out of anything. Weakness: wealth doesn't stop a beam. You still need guards or armor.

### 7b. The Vault

**The fantasy:** You have everything. *Everywhere.* Your caches are fortresses — supplies hidden across the world, each one a small armory, each one trapped, each one known only to you. Sieges don't work on people you supply. Famines don't work. The aliens tried to starve a village once. You opened a cache. Then another. Then another. They stopped trying. You are supply itself.

**Ability kit (6):** hoarder, deep_reserves, scrounger, extra_stomach, squirrel_friend, grave_robber
**Key synergies:** Everywhere (hoarder+deep_reserves) — you are never without
**Gear path:**
- Early: bury things. Remember where.
- Mid: map your caches. Redundancy.
- Late: cache_fortress_kit (acc, T3) — your caches defend themselves
- Godhood: everywhere_key (acc, T4) — one key, every cache, anywhere
**How it plays:** Logistics is your superpower. No one out-supplies you. Weakness: your power is distributed — lose the map, lose everything.

### 7c. The Broker

**The fantasy:** You trade in things others can't: secrets, favors, futures. The galactic economy has a seat for you — not as a merchant, but as a *market maker*. You sold the aliens mining rights to a mountain that doesn't exist, and they thanked you. You hold debts from people who don't know they owe you yet. At the galactic table, everyone owes you something. That's not an accident.

**Ability kit (6):** thief, translator, open_book, bird_whisperer, pact, diplomat
**Key synergies:** Futures Market (pact+open_book) — you trade in what's coming
**Gear path:**
- Early: trade up. Always trade up.
- Mid: brokers_seal (acc, T3) — your mark means the deal is real
- Late: collect debts, not goods
- Godhood: futures_ledger (acc, T4) — you own tomorrow
**How it plays:** You don't win fights. You make fights unnecessary — or profitable. Weakness: a ledger doesn't block a spear. You need people who owe you *protection*.

## 8. Explorer

### 8a. The Farstrider

**The fantasy:** Go anywhere. Know everywhere. Nothing is hidden, nothing is too far. The map is yours. The aliens have star charts spanning galaxies, but they've never *walked* anywhere. You have. Every step of it. When humanity needs to know what's out there, you're already coming back with the answer. At the galactic table, you are the one who's *been* places — and that counts for more than any of them expected.

**Ability kit (6):** wanderer, night_eyes, dowsing, swimmer, tracker, rain_dancer
**Key synergies:** Stormcaller (rain_dancer+dowsing) — the land tells you where water is
**Gear path:**
- Early: good boots, curiosity
- Mid: trail_rations_pouch (acc, T2)
- Late: stormcloak (torso, T3)
- Godhood: farstrider_boots (shoes, T4) + wayfinders_atlas (acc, T4) — distance is a suggestion
**How it plays:** You find the things no one else finds — the hidden caches, the lost villages, the alien crash sites. Weakness: finding the dragon's lair and surviving it are different skills.

### 8b. The Cartographer of Worlds

**The fantasy:** You don't just know the map. You know everywhere that *could* be. You're first to every new place — not because you're fast, but because you know where to look. The aliens map with satellites and drones. You map by *going*. Your atlas has places their computers say don't exist. They're starting to believe the atlas.

**Ability kit (6):** wanderer, dowsing, night_eyes, tracker, echo_location, swimmer
**Key synergies:** True North (dowsing+echo_location) — you always know where *interesting* is
**Gear path:**
- Early: map everything. Everything.
- Mid: horizon_compass (acc, T3) — it points to interesting
- Late: fill in the blanks no one else sees
- Godhood: uncharted_atlas (acc, T4) — maps of places that don't exist yet
**How it plays:** Knowledge is your weapon. You know where everything is — resources, threats, secrets. Weakness: knowing where the treasure is doesn't dig it up.

### 8c. The Pathfinder

**The fantasy:** You make paths where none exist. Others follow in your wake. Every safe road was once your desperate scramble through the dark. Humanity expands because you went first — through the monster territory, across the dead zone, into the alien landing site. They follow the trail you bled to make. At the galactic table, you are humanity's *forward* — the proof that we don't wait for permission to go places.

**Ability kit (6):** wanderer, soft_step, rain_dancer, cold_blooded, hollow_bones, time_skip
**Key synergies:** First Through (soft_step+cold_blooded) — you go where others can't
**Gear path:**
- Early: go first. Always go first.
- Mid: trailblazer_machete (melee, T3) — cut the path
- Late: teach others to follow
- Godhood: first_steps_boots (shoes, T4) — every step is a first
**How it plays:** You open the world for everyone else. Expansion, escape, exploration — you're the tip of the spear. Weakness: going first means facing everything alone, the first time.

## 9. Drifter

### 9a. The Between

**The fantasy:** Untethered. You exist between places — hard to hit, hard to find, impossible to pin down. You were never really *here*. The aliens have tried to capture you, track you, predict you. They've built models. The models say you should be *somewhere*. You're not. At the galactic table, your chair is empty — and everyone understands that this is exactly right.

**Ability kit (6):** time_skip, wanderer, hollow_bones, cold_blooded, soft_step, night_eyes
**Key synergies:** Efficient Machine (cold_blooded+hollow_bones) — you need almost nothing
**Gear path:**
- Early: travel light, keep moving
- Mid: hollow_bone_charm (acc, T2)
- Late: between_boots (shoes, T3), anchors_locket (acc, T3) — always find your way back
- Godhood: phase_shawl (acc, T4) — sometimes you're just... not there when the hit lands
**How it plays:** You can't be caught. Ambushes miss, traps stay empty, and you're gone before the dust settles. Weakness: you can't hold ground. Villages need people who stay.

### 9b. The Unbound

**The fantasy:** No ties. No leverage. Nothing to threaten. You want nothing, so nothing can be taken from you. The aliens can't bargain with you — what would they offer? Can't threaten you — what would they take? Can't buy you — you don't want their currency. You go where the need is, help who needs it, and leave before anyone can thank you. You are the freest thing in the galaxy, and it terrifies everyone who isn't.

**Ability kit (6):** wanderer, cold_blooded, hollow_bones, iron_stomach, deep_reserves, night_eyes

## 10. Caregiver

### 10a. The Undying Ward

**The fantasy:** No one dies on your watch. Wounds close, the sick rise, and your party walks through hell because you're walking with them. The aliens have regeneration tanks, nanite swarms, cloned organs. You have hands, and herbs, and *refusal*. They've watched you work. They don't understand it. Their machines fix bodies. You fix *people*. At the galactic table, you are the reason humanity is still alive to have a seat.

**Ability kit (6):** triage, steady_hands, field_medicine, herbal_remedy, purify, second_wind
**Key synergies:** Surgeon's Calm (triage+steady_hands); Field Pharmacy (field_medicine+herbal_remedy); Clean Plate (purify+iron_stomach)
**Gear path:**
- Early: clean hands, steady nerves
- Mid: field_surgeons_satchel (acc, T2), poultice_bandolier (acc, T2)
- Late: ward_banner (acc, T3)
- Godhood: surgeons_kit (acc, T4) + lifebound_vestments (torso, T4) — you take their wounds as your own and keep standing
**How it plays:** Your party is functionally immortal in any fight they can win at all. You turn close losses into wins. Weakness: you're the priority target, and you can't heal yourself mid-fight as easily.

### 10b. The Renewal

**The fantasy:** You don't just heal. You *restore*. Crippled limbs regrown. Minds unbroken. Villages reborn from ash. The aliens break things — it's what invaders do. You unbreak them. They've offered you positions in their medical corps, their research divisions, their *palaces*. You declined. You have a village to get back to. At the galactic table, you are humanity's answer to entropy itself.

**Ability kit (6):** field_medicine, herbal_remedy, purify, triage, symbiote, second_wind
**Key synergies:** Whole Again (field_medicine+symbiote) — the body remembers how to be whole
**Gear path:**

- Godhood: lifespring_vial (acc, T4) — one drop. That's all it takes.
**How it plays:** Nothing stays broken around you. The party recovers between fights like the fights never happened. Weakness: renewal takes time. You can't do it mid-combat.

### 10c. The Sanctuary

**The fantasy:** Your presence is safety. Disease, poison, despair — they can't touch those under your care. You've held a perimeter of calm in the middle of a monster wave, and the wave broke around you like water around stone. The aliens deploy shield generators, force fields, quarantine protocols. You just *stand there*. And it's enough. At the galactic table, you are the safest place in the galaxy.

**Ability kit (6):** purify, steady_hands, fear_aura, generous, camp_cook, triage
**Key synergies:** Still Point (purify+fear_aura) — calm as a weapon

**How it plays:** You create zones of safety in a dangerous world. Your party fights better because they're not afraid. Weakness: the sanctuary has edges. Outside them, the world is still the world.

## Cross-build notes

- **Hybrid builds are real.** Brawler + Caregiver = the unkillable frontline. Detective + Socialite = the puppetmaster. The 6-slot cap forces choices — that's the game.
- **Multiple paths, one person.** You don't pick a path at character creation. You *discover* it. A socialite might start as a Diplomat and become the Consensus. Paths are destinations, not classes.
- **Sentimental gear** can substitute for any build's armor pieces once bonded (bond ≥25, must be equipped). Love is a valid build path.
- **Alien armor** (5 pieces, tier 4) is the combat builds' end-game armor — but forager/socialite/detective builds have their own tier-4 torso options. No build is forced into alien tech.
- **Most wins look like godhood** — a player who's assembled any of these (or their own invention) by day 60+ is winning. But weird wins exist: the miser who never fights, the drifter who never stays, the detective who wins without drawing a weapon.
- **The galactic table.** Every path above is someone the aliens would respect, fear, recruit, or negotiate with. That's the bar. If a build doesn't clear it, it doesn't belong here.
